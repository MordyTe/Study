# Turn-based and strategy games

- Nothing changes until you act, so observe once, think, then act. Do not wait for stability.
- Read the whole state before the first move: units, resources, the objective, whose turn it is.
- End-of-turn controls are usually a button or a key such as `enter` or `space`. Confirm the turn
  changed in the next observation (a counter, a highlight, a different active unit).
- Keep a note per important rule you discover ("archers cannot move after attacking").
- When many identical units exist, act on the one nearest the position you mean; refs for twins
  resolve by position.
